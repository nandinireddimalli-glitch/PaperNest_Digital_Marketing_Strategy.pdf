# 📚 PaperNest – Digital Marketing Strategy

> A digital marketing strategy project created as part of my Digital Marketing Internship at Veda Technology.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**PaperNest** is a fictional personalized stationery brand created for a digital marketing strategy project.

The project focuses on developing a practical digital marketing plan to build brand awareness, engage the target audience, increase online visibility, and support potential sales.

The strategy combines audience research, measurable marketing goals, digital channel selection, social media content planning, creative design, and KPI tracking.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Build awareness for the PaperNest brand
- Define a clear target audience
- Develop measurable digital marketing goals
- Select appropriate digital marketing channels
- Create a weekly social media content plan
- Design a sample Instagram marketing creative
- Identify relevant marketing KPIs
- Create a basic KPI tracking framework

---

## 🏷️ About PaperNest

PaperNest is a fictional stationery brand offering personalized and creative stationery products such as:

- 📓 Customized notebooks
- 📔 Journals
- 📅 Planners
- 📝 Sticky notes
- 🎁 Stationery gift sets

### Brand Vision

To inspire creativity and productivity through personalized stationery.

### Brand Mission

To provide stylish, affordable and customizable stationery products that reflect customers' personalities and needs.

### Brand Tagline

**"Your Ideas. Your Style. Your PaperNest."**

---

## 👥 Target Audience

The primary target audience includes:

- **Age:** 16–30 years
- **Location:** Urban and semi-urban areas in India
- **Audience:** Students, young professionals and stationery enthusiasts
- **Interests:** Journaling, studying, productivity, creativity, planning and aesthetic stationery
- **Preferred Platforms:** Instagram, Pinterest, YouTube and WhatsApp

### Customer Persona

**Name:** Aanya  
**Age:** 21  
**Occupation:** College Student  
**Location:** Hyderabad, India

Aanya is interested in journaling, studying, productivity, aesthetic desk setups and personalized stationery.

She discovers products through social media, recommendations, Pinterest and online content.

## 📊 Marketing Goals

The strategy includes the following initial three-month planning targets:

| Objective | Target |
|---|---:|
| Instagram Reach | 10,000 users |
| Instagram Followers | 1,000 |
| Average Engagement Rate | 5% |
| Website Visits | 500/month by Month 3 |
| Online Orders | 50 |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | 15% |

> These are planning targets for the fictional PaperNest project and do not represent actual business results.
